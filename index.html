Transparency Check

A fixed-fee check of your firm's website against the SRA Transparency Rules.

The SRA requires firms to publish price and service information for nine specified areas of work, along with a complaints procedure that tells clients how and when to go to the Legal Ombudsman. The rules are detailed, and it is easy to be most of the way there without being all of the way there.

The SRA checks. It runs periodic sweeps of firm websites, and has issued hundreds of warnings and a number of fixed penalties. The penalty for a transparency breach is £750 for a first finding and £1,500 after that.

What you get

  • Every page of your site read against Rules 1.3, 1.4, 2.1 and 4
  • A list of what is missing, quoting your own wording and linking the exact page
  • Suggested replacement wording you can paste in
  • A note of what you already do correctly, so you are not fixing things that are fine

One firm, one fixed fee. If the check finds nothing that needs changing, there is nothing to pay.

How it works

The check is largely automated — that is what makes a fixed fee possible at this price. Every finding is then read and confirmed by hand before it reaches you, because a compliance report that cries wolf is worse than no report at all. Findings that cannot be confirmed are reported as "worth checking", not as breaches.

What this is not

This is not legal advice, and I am not a solicitor. It is a careful reading of a public website against a published rulebook. Where something is a judgement call, it is flagged as one rather than asserted. Nothing here is affiliated with or endorsed by the Solicitors Regulation Authority.
