What we check

Four rules, read line by line

The SRA requires published price and service information for nine areas of work, and a complaints procedure that names the Legal Ombudsman. The requirements are detailed. Firms rarely fail all of them. They often fail one.

The SRA checks too. It sweeps firm websites periodically and has issued hundreds of warnings alongside a number of fixed penalties.

How the review is done

Automated reading, checked by hand

Software reads the site first. That is what keeps the fee at £149 rather than a consultancy day rate.

Then every finding is verified against the live page before it goes anywhere near you. During testing the software produced five findings that turned out to be wrong. All five were caught at this step. None reached a firm.

Anything that cannot be verified is reported as worth checking, not as a breach.

  1. Your site is crawled and every page bearing on the rules is collected.
  2. Each requirement is assessed against what those pages actually say.
  3. Findings are confirmed by hand against the live page, and unverifiable ones are downgraded.
  4. You get the report: what fails, what passes, and replacement wording for the gaps.
